The "Emergants" or "Structs" in "Kybernautics" [242](242.md) is a series of Metaphorical Objects which are implemented to study how Information is distributed as structures throughout Spaces and Domains [[262]]

Of the structs, a single point is defined as a "Kybnete [239](239.md)"  When two (or more) Kybnetes join to form Extremes or Parallels, an emergent structure unfolds; the Continuum [50](50.md) or in Kybernautic Taxonomy [[263]] the "Apeixos"
