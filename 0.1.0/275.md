The purpose of Knowledge as "Cartography of Ontology."

[2] Ancient traditions warn against the adoration of Knowledge; like all else in this Universe, nothing in isolation can survive. Knowledge, must be qualified and subject to these same standards. **Knowledge then, integrated into Human life, serves what purpose that another object cannot fulfill?** That is the primary Thesis of this study.

3, Knowledge itself differs from information, in that knowledge is "Information processed by a Human-being." Knowledge is therefore, not merely a natural process; it is a deliberate, conscious process. 

Knowledge, however, risks the "Mandelbrot problem" [276](276.md) 

Knowledge, is equivalent to mapping, and mapping is the visibility of terrain. Every field has some kind of mapping; why? We must turn the objects of the intangible, into manipulable structure, while embedding source truth. Visibility is invariant to solving problems, understanding, and seeing. To witness is the precursor of Human autonomy.
