"Mathematics" or simply "Math" is the study of Quantity[130](0.1.0/130.md), Transformation[123](0.1.0/123.md) and Change in Systems[38](0.1.0/38.md) and Problems[93](0.1.0/93.md). Mathematics studies these primary elements diverse applications, purposes and enviornments [[83]]. 

Mathematics is able to measure these elements of change, by the implementation of Units of Measurement [[53]] and Transformations.

Mathematics expresses types of Transformation [[123]] and Change in the form of Symbols [[]] and Quantity expressed as Number [[]] formed the "Language of Mathematics, a Language describing precise change."

Mathematics is a Fundamental Subject [[33]] with implications spanning just about every part of everyday life [[83]]
