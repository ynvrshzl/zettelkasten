**Irritability is one of the most problematic Physiological states.**

Mythologically, Irritability could be Phenomenologically [[173]] described as "an inverted force-field where everything around you just  appears to go spectacularly wrong." Not only are you low on energy; but your very soul is siphoned of it's respite; nothing appears to be worth the struggle; that is precisely the physiological soup of anhedonia, apathy, lethargy and exhaustion; futilism.

Ultimately what remains of a Human being; whom is devoid of meaning. **"Sometimes, we walk through the Abyss, not by sight, sound, feel or sense... but by faith."**

It is one of the most native; clear-to-life Phenomenological accounts; this is demonstrably important.

This is one of the oldest parts of Human Nature [[]] and it goes beyond the Materialistic Dismemberment of Religion [[]]

I think the only strategy to avoiding irritability-insanity; is to apply the principles by "Father Angelo, The most irritable of the Cynics."

"I just wish there was more time. I wish I could externalize"
