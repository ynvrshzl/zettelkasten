###### title
Orthogonomics

###### Author
Lzahlia's [[55]]
###### Descriptor
The central proposition is **"There is a proper way to approach life; a language to all things."** The proceeding idea draws from Kybernautic Principles of Information [[269]]: "Pacing, Density or Slow and Steady."

###### 

This field directly proceeds the field of Orthogony; **"The Universe suggests a Cohesive, Underlying structure; A clockwork not of material logic, but of Dynamics and Harmony, and perhaps a universal law of 'Good.' Our task is to discover, and align every Domain in our being towards it."**

Orthogonomics is not a field about "Optimizing workflows," it is a field of study of Orthogonality: "There is a correct way to do things." Ergonomics explores "The effective approach to all things, guiding how we may approach the non-finite, with our finite resources."

Two major ideas: Orthogonality as in "Correct" and Orthogonality as in "Connected Structurally."

In some sense, most fields, from Mathematics to Music-Theory, we are discovering how to properly do something. In the case of music, one may learn by natural-ability, or by merely tinkering or even brute-forcing musicality. This however, is neither economic nor sustainable; eventually one must learn what works and what does not.

These fields provide high-entropy-feedback; you know exactly when something goes wrong. Other parts of life are less tangible

However, a necessary limit must be introduced; while there may be an ergonomic approach to all things, we simply do not have the necessary time or resources to explore these problems. "We are not limited by space, but by time."
